TRACK RECORD

Two decades of policy wins, from the coast to Congress

Carbon to Sea Initiative

Global policy and communications lead

Led the global policy and communications function for a $70 million philanthropic effort shaping governance for ocean-based carbon dioxide removal. Worked with scientists, entrepreneurs, nonprofit leaders and policymakers, using grant-making to build policymakers’ understanding of new approaches to climate action, and helped Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution secure the first EPA research permit in the field.

Oceana

Campaign Director, Climate and Energy

Led the U.S. Climate and Energy team and its winning campaign to stop the expansion of offshore drilling. The campaign won support from more than 300 municipal governments and every East and West Coast governor, passed bipartisan legislation in 10 states and the U.S. House, and secured a 10-year drilling moratorium for four states.

The Pew Charitable Trusts

Campaign director

Directed a successful campaign to reform the Army Corps of Engineers’ Nationwide Permit program, securing the first-ever national living shorelines permit and opening the door to nature-based solutions to coastal erosion.

Earlier career

Conservation, government and finance

Director of external affairs and government relations at Restore America’s Estuaries; roles on The Nature Conservancy’s Climate Change, Federal Legislative Campaigns and EcoEnterprises Fund teams, including co-writing a book on environmental venture capital in Latin America. Also a local elected official in Washington, DC, a program manager at the District Department of Environment, an AmeriCorps member, and a microfinance worker in West Africa. B.S. summa cum laude, Agricultural and Resource Economics, University of Maryland.
